# Phase 3.2-B Commercial Re-ranking Methodology

Priority order:
1. Direct industrial automation engineering relevance.
2. Buyer intent such as adoption, design, selection, cost, quotation, ROI, retrofit, PoC, FAT, Cycle Time, and interlock review.
3. Industry vertical fit with Ajin Networks' target domains.
4. SEO/content repair opportunity.
5. Recency.
6. Strong exclusion penalty for trend-jacking topics such as politics, stocks, sports, games, consumer finance, and unrelated current affairs.

The score is a repository-content heuristic. It is not a substitute for Google Search Console or CRM lead data.
